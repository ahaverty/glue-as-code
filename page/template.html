Don't believe it? Change it.

    

      
Speed it up
Tap Edit, change setcpm(130/4) to setcpm(174/4), then Run. Now it's drum and bass.

      
Change the harmony
Swap Fm9 in the prog line for F^7. The pads, stabs and arp all follow, because they all read that one line.

      
Rewrite the beat
In the kick line, each ~ is a silent 16th note. Replace every ~ with c2 for four to the floor.

How is this possible?

    
Strudel is a free, open-source live-coding language that runs in the browser. It's a JavaScript port of TidalCycles, which people use to perform electronic music live by typing.

    
Patterns in quotes are rhythms: "x ~ ~ x ~ ~ x ~" splits a bar into eight steps and plays on the x's. < > means one item per bar, which is how the four chords cycle. Everything after a dot shapes the sound: .lpf() is a low-pass filter, .room() is reverb, .delay() is an echo.

    
The drums are maths too. The kick is a sine wave whose pitch drops fast (penv). The hats are white noise through a high-pass filter. The clap is pink noise through a band-pass filter.
